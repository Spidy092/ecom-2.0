# S06 — Round 2 shopper observation

- **Participant ID:** S06
- **Group:** Shopper
- **Mode:** First-time
- **Viewport:** 412 × 924 CSS px
- **Recording consent:** No recording retained

## Direct observations / feedback

- The participant described the experience as simple and good.
- The participant understood Cart and Saved for later without facilitator guidance.
- The participant liked that Saved stays out of the main shopping surface until they choose the Saved navigation item; they said this kept the interface from feeling crowded.
- The participant typed `tomoto` when looking for Tomato and initially believed Tomato was unavailable.

## Product implication

Keep the separate, on-demand Saved surface and the revised Cart/Saved terminology. Add spelling-recovery to the next search research question: test whether shoppers need a clearer no-result recovery message or typo suggestion. Do not add an unbounded fuzzy-search feature based on one observation.
