# Buyer onboarding acceptance gate

The engineering flow is not commercially validated until a real store owner can complete the following without developer intervention:

- find Store Setup / Personalize Store;
- add or replace the site logo/title;
- choose a supplied style variation;
- change a brand color;
- edit header/footer/navigation;
- replace at least one demo product image;
- identify where WooCommerce shipping/payment settings live;
- preview the storefront on mobile and desktop;
- explain what remains before launch.

## Moderated test target

For the first commercial gate, test at least five relevant WordPress/WooCommerce store owners or implementers. Record:

- task completion;
- time-to-first-success;
- navigation mistakes;
- wording that caused confusion;
- whether they believed the product modified content without permission;
- whether they understood demo images/products were replaceable;
- whether they understood presentation completion is not legal/payment/shipping certification.

Do not convert observations into marketing claims until the evidence exists.
